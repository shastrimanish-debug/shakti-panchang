Privacy Policy – Shakti Panchang

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Shakti Panchang is provided by me, Manish Kumar Shastri. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use its features, including Panchang, Kundali, location services, reminders, the Uma voice assistant, and Premium subscriptions.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

To ensure smooth functionality, the app stores saved locations, selected settings, Kundali profiles, and reminder preferences locally on your device. This data is not automatically uploaded to me or my servers simply by virtue of being stored on your phone.

2. Location Services

If you choose to use GPS or your current location, the app will request device location permissions. Your coordinates may be sent securely over HTTPS to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service to retrieve the correct place name. If you search for a location not available in the offline database, your search query may also be sent to this service. Alternatively, you can deny GPS permissions and manually select a location from the saved list.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition

The voice assistant, Uma, requires microphone access when you initiate voice input. Speech recognition may be processed by your device's operating system or its configured speech-recognition provider. The app only listens when you activate the feature; it does not intentionally record continuous background audio.

4. Uma and Online Services

Core Panchang calculations are performed entirely offline within the app. However, if an online AI or network-dependent feature is enabled, the necessary information required to process your request may be transmitted to the respective service. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not required for the feature you are using.

5. Google Play Subscriptions

Shakti Panchang Premium is offered through Google Play Billing. Google securely handles your payment information, including credit cards and UPI. The app never receives or stores your payment details. I only receive your purchase or subscription status to activate the Premium features. Google Play’s own privacy policy governs all payment processing.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

I do not sell your personal information. Data is only transmitted to third-party services (such as geocoding, speech recognition, or AI functionality) when you actively use features that require them.

7. Security and Data Deletion

All network communications made by the app are secured using HTTPS. Locally stored data can be cleared using the app's reset/delete features or simply by uninstalling the app, subject to standard Android storage behaviors. For privacy inquiries or requests to delete data held by my services, please contact me.

8. Children's Privacy

Shakti Panchang is a general-purpose application and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in app features or data handling practices. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated whenever material changes are made.

10. Contact Me

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact me via my website: myshivshakti.in.
