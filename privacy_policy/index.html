Privacy Policy – Shakti Panchang

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Shakti Panchang is provided by SHIV SHAKTI. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use Panchang, Kundali, location, reminders, Uma voice assistance and Premium subscription features.

1. Information stored on your device

The app can store saved locations, selected settings, Kundali profiles and reminder settings locally on your device so that the features continue to work. These records are not automatically uploaded to SHIV SHAKTI merely because they are stored locally.

2. Location

If you choose to use GPS/current location, the app requests device location permission. Coordinates can be sent over HTTPS to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service to determine a place name. If you search for a place not present in the app's offline list, the search text can also be sent to that service. You can instead use a manually selected/saved location and deny GPS permission.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Uma can use the microphone when you start voice input. Speech recognition may be processed by the operating system or its configured speech-recognition provider. The app does not intentionally record continuous microphone audio in the background.

4. Uma and online services

Core Panchang calculations run in the app. If an online AI or other network-backed feature is enabled in a particular build, the information required for that request may be transmitted to the relevant service. Do not enter sensitive information that is not necessary for the feature you are using.

5. Google Play subscription

Shakti Panchang Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the user's card, UPI and other payment-instrument information. The app does not receive those payment-instrument details. The app can receive purchase/subscription status needed to determine whether the Premium product is active. Google Play's own privacy terms apply to payment processing.

6. Sharing and sale

SHIV SHAKTI does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to the third-party services described above when you actively use the related feature, such as geocoding, speech recognition or online AI functionality.

7. Security and deletion

Network requests used by the app are intended to use HTTPS. Locally stored records can be removed using the app's available delete/reset features or by uninstalling the app, subject to Android's storage behavior. You may contact SHIV SHAKTI for privacy questions or deletion requests relating to information held by a service.

8. Children

Shakti Panchang is a general-purpose app and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data handling change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact SHIV SHAKTI through myshivshakti.in.
